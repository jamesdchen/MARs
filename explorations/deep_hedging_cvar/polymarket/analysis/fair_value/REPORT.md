# Polymarket crypto Up/Down as short-dated digitals: fair value and hedging

**Every number in this report comes from the TRAIN split only.** That is the
431 markets (248 × 5m, 146 × 15m, 37 × 4h, across 8 coins) whose whole
`[open, end]` window lies inside 2026-08-21 00:00 – 2026-08-23 00:00 UTC.

- Binance 1 s data is read only for times before 2026-08-23 00:00.
  2026-08-20 is used only to warm up the EWMA vol.
- The loaders in `fair_value.py` filter rows with pyarrow filters when they
  read the files, and then assert the bounds. Rows from Aug 23 (validation)
  and Aug 24 (test) are never loaded.

Run the scripts in this order:
1. `q1_resolution.py`
2. `build_panel.py`
3. `q2_fair_value.py`
4. `q3_leadlag.py`
5. `q4_adverse_selection.py`
6. `q5_hedging.py`

Outputs go to `cache/` (git-ignored) and `figures/`. The pricer is in
`fair_value.py`: `digital_up_prob`, `digital_up_delta`, `ewma_sigma`,
`model_at`.

## 1. Resolution rule

The gamma description says the market resolves "to Up if the TWAP of <COIN>,
generated by Chainlink, of the time range in the title is greater than or
equal to the price at the beginning of that range". The source is
`data.chain.link/streams/<coin>-usd-twap-60s-streams`, with
`twapLookbackSeconds=60` for every coin and contract length.

**Rule: Up ⇔ TWAP60(end) ≥ TWAP60(start).**
- `TWAP60(T)` is the stream value at `T`, i.e. the average over `[T−60 s, T]`.
- Both sides of the comparison are 60 s TWAPs.
- **Ties resolve Up.**

| check | result |
|---|---|
| winner == (finalPrice ≥ priceToBeat) | 417/417 markets with metadata (14 have none) |
| exact ties | 0 |
| priceToBeat(start=T) == finalPrice(end=T), same coin | 58/58 pairs, bit-identical |
| best Binance proxy (residual sd after a per-coin level) | TWAP60 shifted −4 s: 1.78 bp. Unshifted: 2.17 bp. TWAP30: 6.3 bp. Spot at T: 12.7 bp |
| level basis ln(Chainlink/Binance), median | −1.9 to −2.7 bp for the spot coins (≈ USDT premium); −8.3 bp for HYPE (perp proxy) |
| sd of the basis drift over the window | 5m 1.9 bp, 15m 1.8 bp, 4h 2.3 bp. For scale, Chainlink return sd: 46 / 93 / 255 bp |
| Binance-TWAP outcome == Chainlink outcome | 5m 238/240 (both flips had \|ret\| < 0.5 bp), 15m 140/140, 4h 37/37 |

So the strike `K` is known in full at open, and there is no case where it is
still forming. The model uses `K` = Binance TWAP60 at open, so the level basis
cancels out.

Fees: crypto_fees_v2 with rate 0.07 and exponent 1, charged to takers only,
with a 20% maker rebate. The tick is 0.001.

## 2. Fair-value model

```
p = Φ( ln(F/K)/√V − √V/2 )
```

- For `τ ≥ 60 s`: `F = S` and `V = σ²(τ − 60 + 60/3)`.
- For `τ < 60 s`: `F = wM + (1−w)S` and `V = σ²τ³/(3·60²)`, where
  - `M` is the part of the closing TWAP already observed;
  - `w = (60−τ)/60`.
- `S` is the last closed Binance 1 s kline, so every input is causal.

**Choosing σ.** The grid was half-life {60, 300, 900, 3600} s × return
sampling {1, 5, 30, 60} s × shift {0, −4} s.
- Short half-lives win: 60–300 s gives log loss 0.301–0.302, against
  0.310–0.313 for 3600 s.
- 30 s sampling beats 1 s, because Binance 1 s returns understate the
  variance over the horizon (variance ratio at 30 s is 1.3–1.9 for the spot
  coins).
- The −4 s shift does not help.
- **Chosen:** half-life 300 s on overlapping 30 s returns. It is within
  0.0003 nats of the best setting and less noisy.
- A Student-t with ν = 4 (unit variance) beats the Gaussian: 0.2993 vs 0.3015.
  The best vol multiplier is 1.0.

**How the scores are computed.**
- Log loss (LL) clips `p` to `[0.001, 0.999]`.
- Each market counts once per time-to-close bucket. "All" is the equal-weight
  mean over buckets.
- The blend is a logistic regression in logit space, fitted 2-fold by day.

## 3. Lead-lag from Binance to Polymarket

The sample is consecutive two-sided snapshot pairs no more than 20 s apart,
leaving out the last 30 s of each market: 17k pairs (5m), 32k (15m) and 80k
(4h). The median gap between snapshots is 1–4 s. Figure: `q3_leadlag.png`.

| | 5m | 15m | 4h |
|---|---|---|---|
| lag of the cross-correlation peak (ρ) | 0 s (0.67) | 0 s (0.39) | 0 s (0.56) |
| pass-through at lag 0 / by 5 s / total | 85% / 87% / 0.92 | 81% / 89% / 0.89 | 73% / 86% / 0.81 |
| 90% passed through by | 12 s | 9 s | 10 s |
| R² of Δmid(5 s) on the gap (slope) | 0.6% (0.09) | 8.2% (0.28) | 0.6% (0.02) |
| R² of Δmid(30 s) on the gap (slope) | 0.9% (0.26) | 2.6% (0.26) | 0.9% (0.04) |
| slope of Δp(5 s) on the gap | −0.17 | −0.07 | −0.00 |
| gap half-life | 10 s | 5 s | 270 s (mean gap +1.8 c) |

- **No whole-second lag at snapshot resolution.** 73–85% of a Binance move
  reaches the mid within the same second; the remaining 10–25% arrives over
  about 10 s.
- **The gap barely predicts the next mid move:** under 1% of variance for 5m
  and 4h, 8% for 15m at 5 s.
- **In 5m**, part of the gap is Binance noise that reverts.
- **In 4h** the gap is a persistent disagreement about the level, not a lag.

## 4. Adverse selection

The sample is 158.9k taker fills inside the market windows (5m 107k, 15m 45k,
4h 6.5k), about $1.6M notional, from the data-api with `takerOnly`. The
pre-trade state is read at block time − 2 s.

- P(taker buys Up) rises from 0.36 when the gap is below −10 c to 0.60 (5m),
  0.71 (15m) and 0.62 (4h) when it is above +10 c.
- The share of fills that agree with the sign of the gap is 56%, 58% and 59%.

Maker markout against the mid, count-weighted, in c/share. "Agree" means the
taker traded in the direction of the gap.

| length | 5 s (agree / disagree) | 30 s (agree / disagree) | settlement (agree / disagree) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15m | −0.18 / +0.19 | −0.14 / +0.21 | −0.1 / −1.0 |
| 4h | −0.11 / +0.24 | −0.51 / +0.40 | −9.7 / +12.0 |
| 5m | +0.74 / −0.37 | +1.20 / −1.55 | −1.4 / +0.4 |

In 15m and 4h, the takers who agree with the gap are toxic. 5m goes the other
way at 5–30 s, which is consistent with noise in the gap.

**Maker simulation.** A unit-size maker is filled whenever a taker's price
crosses its quote. Half-spread 1 c. Values are c/fill.

| quote centre | vs settlement: all | 5m | 15m | 4h | vs mid at +60 s: all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mid | −5.9 | −7.4 | −2.9 | −1.6 | −3.4 |
| model p | −3.3 | −3.5 | −4.2 | +8.1 | −2.5 |
| ½(mid + p) | −2.7 | −3.7 | −1.4 | +8.1 | −1.8 |

Per market, centring quotes on `p` instead of the mid changes P&L by:
- all markets: +$5.0 against settlement (CI [−1.3, +11.4]) and +$1.4 against
  the mid at +60 s (CI [−0.1, +3.1]);
- 4h only: +$6.8 against settlement (CI [+1.9, +12.5]) but −$1.9 against the
  mid at +60 s. This is the persistent-gap issue from §3.

This shows a direction, not a significant result. A maker centred on the mid
gives up about 2–3 c per fill. That is the size of the half-spread (median
quoted spread 2–3 c) plus the taker fee 0.07·p(1−p), which is about 1.75 c at
p = 0.5.

## 5. Hedging economics

**Size of the delta.** At the median train σ (90%/yr), the USD delta per Up
share is:

| length | at open (ATM) | closer to expiry (ATM) |
|---|---|---|
| 5m | $205 | 150 s left: $316; 30 s left: $1,046 |
| 15m | $113 | – |
| 4h | $28 | 1 h left: $56 |

Inside the TWAP window, delta falls in proportion to τ/60. The realised p99
reaches $800–1,500 near close for 5m and 15m.

**Backtest setup.**
- Hold +1 Up share, bought at model `p`, until settlement.
- Hedge by shorting Δ coins at Binance 1 s prices, paying fees on every
  rebalance and on the unwind.
- Synthetic windows: every aligned slot of Aug 21–22 (n = 4,608 for 5m,
  1,536 for 15m, 360 hourly-start windows for 4h), settled on the Binance
  TWAP. The actual markets settle on Chainlink.
- Figures: `q5_delta_surface.png`, `q5_hedge_frontier.png`.

| synthetic, entry at open | unhedged sd / CVaR95 | hedge every 1 s, gross sd | fees for 1 s hedging (2 bp / 5 bp) | best net at 2 bp: sd / CVaR95 / fees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5m | 0.494 / 0.668 | 0.161 | 55 c / 139 c | capped-30 s: 0.287 / 0.770 / 12 c |
| 15m | 0.496 / 0.582 | 0.143 | 54 c / 135 c | capped-30 s: 0.272 / 0.690 / 10 c |
| 4h | 0.484 / 0.515 | 0.160 | 56 c / 141 c | capped-30 s: 0.257 / 0.608 / 8 c |
| 4h, entry with 25% left | 0.320 / 0.763 | – | – | capped-30 s: 0.168 / 0.469 / 4.3 c (taker fees: 0.202 / 0.660 / 10.7 c) |

"Capped-30 s" means: rebalance every 30 s, cap |Δ| at the ATM delta at entry,
and go flat for the last 60 s.

Findings:
- **Hedging more often makes the tail worse.** Hedging every 1 s or 5 s, or
  with 5–25% no-trade bands, raises CVaR95 in every set: by 2–6 $/share at
  taker fees.
- **Late 4h entries are the exception.** On the actual 4h markets entered with
  25% of life left, CVaR95 falls from 0.81 to 0.29 for 2.6 c (rebalance every
  120 s, maker fees, n = 37).
- **5m and 15m:** hedging costs 6–30 c/share against a maker edge of about
  1–1.5 c, and CVaR95 never improves.
- **Basis risk is small.** Chainlink can't be watched live; it is only seen
  through `priceToBeat` and `finalPrice`. On actual 5m markets, the net hedged
  sd is 0.458 with the Chainlink outcome vs 0.432 with the Binance outcome
  (rebalance every 5 s, 2 bp). 15m and 4h are identical. The basis drifts
  about 2 bp per window.
- **Hedging across contract lengths doesn't pay.**
  - Correlations of 5 s changes in model `p`: 5m~15m 0.65, 5m~4h 0.72,
    15m~4h 0.77. Empirical mid correlations: 0.55, 0.42, 0.33.
  - A static hedge of 5m Up with 4h Up needs a median of 7.3 shares (p90 10).
    It lowers sd from 0.494 to 0.395 but raises CVaR95 from 0.668 to 0.800.
    Hedging with 15m is worse (sd 0.93).
  - Each leg costs about 7 × (1–1.5 c spread + 1.75 c fee) ≈ 20–25 c, vs
    about 4 c on the perp. Use it only as free netting.

## 6. Conclusions for the RL environment

1. **Observation features:**
   - `p_model` (Student-t ν=4, EWMA half-life 300 s on 30 s returns);
   - `gap = p_model − mid` and its change over 5–10 s (the gap half-life is
     5–10 s in 5m and 15m; in 4h the gap persists);
   - `z = ln(F/K)/√V`;
   - `σ√V` in bp;
   - `tau/T`, plus a flag for being inside the closing TWAP window;
   - `delta_usd` per share, and inventory × `delta_usd`;
   - 5 s and 30 s Binance returns.

   No Chainlink feature is needed: the basis is about ±2 bp and `K` is known.
2. **Hedge action:**
   - 5m and 15m: none.
   - 4h: optional. Make it a coarse "hedge to fraction f of model delta"
     action with 2 bp fees, capped at 1× the ATM delta, and flat for the last
     60 s. That gives 50–75% lower sd and 20–60% lower CVaR95, for
     3–8 c/share.
   - Hedge the net delta per coin.
3. **Baseline quoter:**
   - Centre: `c = clip(½(p_model + mid), 0.01, 0.99)`. For 5m, use the logit
     blend `0.6·logit p + 0.32·logit mid`.
   - Half-spread: `h = max(tick, 1 c, ½·market spread) + 0.5·|gap|`.
   - Inventory skew: `−k·q`.
   - Pull quotes when `tau < 30 s` (5m and 15m) or when `|gap| > 10 c`.
   - Compare it with a mid-centred quoter using the same `h`: on train,
     −2.7 vs −5.9 c/fill against settlement.

## Caveats

- **Model selection:** the model was chosen in-sample on train.
- **Small 4h sample:** 4h rests on 37 markets from two trending days.
- **Timing:** snapshot timing is limited by the collector, and trade times
  are block times.
- **Maker simulation:** it ignores queue position and size, and its P&L
  against settlement is noisy, so rely on the market-bootstrap CIs.
- **Fees:** the taker fee formula is assumed (it matches the docs, but no
  per-trade fee was verified).
